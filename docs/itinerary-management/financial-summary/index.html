Financial Summary

View automated financial calculations at the bottom of the Itinerary tab.

Displayed Calculations​

📊 Total Cost​

Formula: Sum of all Unit Cost values from all financial lines

Represents: Total cost for this booking

Includes:

  • Flight costs
  • Hotel costs
  • others costs
  • Supplement costs

💰 Total Price​

Formula: Sum of all Total Price values from all financial lines

Represents: Total amount charged to customer

Includes:

  • Base costs
  • Markups
  • VAT/taxes
  • All supplements

💵 Total Profit Amount​

Formula: Total Price − Total Cost

Represents: Absolute profit for this booking

Example:

Total Price: $5,000
Total Cost: $4,000
Total Profit Amount: $1,000

📈 Total Profit Percentage​

Formula: (Total Profit Amount ÷ Total Price) × 100

Represents: Profit margin as a percentage

Example:

Total Profit Amount: $1,000
Total Price: $5,000
Total Profit Percentage: 20%

financialSummery.png

Real-Time Updates​

The financial summary updates automatically when you:

  • Add new financial lines
  • Modify existing costs
  • Change markup percentages
  • Adjust VAT rates
  • Update quantities

Verification​

Use the financial summary to:

  • ✅ Verify total booking cost
  • ✅ Check profit margins
  • ✅ Ensure pricing meets targets
  • ✅ Compare against quotes
  • ✅ Review before finalizing

Best Practices​

✅ Review summary before confirming bookings
✅ Ensure profit margins meet company targets
✅ Verify all costs are included
✅ Check for missing financial lines
✅ Compare with budget estimates
